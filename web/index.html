🛡️ Security Watch

Small shops can't watch CCTV all day. Point a camera at the room and this page notices movement, spots people while the shop is closed and knives or bats at any time, records the clip and pings your phone on Telegram.

🐍 Written in Python (OpenCV + NumPy), running right here in your browser with Pyodide.

🔒 Privacy: the video is analysed on this device and is never uploaded. Clips stay in this tab until you download them. The only thing that leaves the device is the Telegram alert you set up yourself, sent straight to your own bot. Tell people when a camera is recording.

How it works

  1. Background subtraction: OpenCV's MOG2 learns what the empty room looks like and marks anything that moves (grey boxes). Shadows are ignored.
  2. Person and weapon detection: when something moves, a NanoDet network (trained on the 80 everyday COCO objects) looks for people, knives and bats. You can switch to OpenCV's classic HOG people detector, which is faster.
  3. Alarm rules: a person while the alarm is on (always, or only outside opening hours), or a weapon at any time, raises an alarm. Repeats of the same alarm wait a minute.
  4. Video saving: each alarm records the annotated video until the room has been quiet for 6 seconds.
  5. Telegram alert: a snapshot, and then the clip, are sent to your phone through your own Telegram bot.

The detection is Python (watch.py) running in your browser through Pyodide, in a background thread so the video stays smooth. Guns are not in the COCO objects, so this demo cannot reliably spot them; see the README. See the code on GitHub. Sample video: Intel IoT DevKit sample videos (CC BY 4.0).
